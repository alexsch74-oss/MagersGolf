After the missing pandemic years, the Cup resumed in Uppsala. Upsala Golfklubb dates to 1937; Magersfontein returned after a three-year gap with thirteen years of unfinished business behind it.

England created a 4-point gap over the weekend — enough to settle the Cup without making the losing side disappear from the story.
