2013 · Saunton

England found the separation.

England 10.5 – 5.5 Sweden
Home captain: John Mitchell · Away captain: Matte Eriksson

Saunton has hosted golf since the nineteenth century. Herbert Fowler shaped the East Course in the 1920s, and the club’s links history gives this Cup a pleasing contrast: ancient golfing ground, very young rivalry.

England created a 5-point gap over the weekend — enough to settle the Cup without making the losing side disappear from the story.

The final score is only the surface. The format splits below show where the weekend moved, while the full match record preserves the awkward detail.
