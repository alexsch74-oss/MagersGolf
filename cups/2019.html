2019 · Old Thorns

No separation. Plenty of consequence.

England 8 – 8 Sweden
Home captain: Alex Scheepers · Away captain: Matte Eriksson

Old Thorns’ course was designed by Peter Alliss and opened with a first round featuring Jack Nicklaus, Seve Ballesteros, Isao Aoki and Bill Rogers. In 2019, the venue got another team contest — and another draw.

England and Sweden finished level at 8–8. In Magersfontein terms, a draw is not neutral: the holders keep the Cup.

The final score is only the surface. The format splits below show where the weekend moved, while the full match record preserves the awkward detail.
