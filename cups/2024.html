2024 · Landskrona

A decisive Sweden weekend.

Sweden 11 – 5 England
Home captain: Björn Ekholm · Away captain: Graham Carter

Landskrona Golfklubb was founded in 1960 at Erikstorp, north of Borstahusen. The 2024 Cup used Erikstorp and Hildesborg and gave Sweden one of the clearest victories in the modern record.

Sweden won by 6 points, one of the clearer verdicts in the Cup record.

The final score is only the surface. The format splits below show where the weekend moved, while the full match record preserves the awkward detail.
