2012 · Barsebäck

Close enough to remain personal.

Sweden 7 – 9 England
Home captain: Björn Ekholm · Away captain: Martin Turner

By year three the event had acquired repetition, expectation and a past. Barsebäck gave the Cup a Scandinavian stage with enough scale to make the weekend feel larger than its headcount.

England won by 2 points. This is the kind of margin that keeps individual holes alive in conversation for years.

The final score is only the surface. The format splits below show where the weekend moved, while the full match record preserves the awkward detail.
