2011 · Celtic Manor

A decisive England weekend.

England 11 – 5 Sweden
Home captain: Graham Carter · Away captain: Tomas Persson

A year after the 2010 Ryder Cup, Magersfontein borrowed one of team golf’s grandest stages. Celtic Manor’s Twenty Ten course had been created specifically for Ryder Cup theatre. The audience was smaller; the arguments were not.

England won by 6 points, one of the clearer verdicts in the Cup record.

The final score is only the surface. The format splits below show where the weekend moved, while the full match record preserves the awkward detail.
