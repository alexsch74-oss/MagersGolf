Stories hiding in the data

The BetterBall specialists

Some players improve when somebody else is sharing the problem. Player pages now compare format points-per-match, making partnership golf and Singles genuinely different reputations.

The Sunday problem

Singles strips away the partner and makes the record personal. The new profiles expose who thrives alone and who would prefer Saturday never ended.

The recurring opponent

Because every player page now carries opponent records, the Cup can identify genuine bogey opponents rather than relying on selective recollection.

Home advantage

There have been 9 outright home wins and 2 outright away wins. The venue matters, but not enough to be trusted blindly.

Largest verdict

2011 at Celtic Manor: England won by 6 points.

Retention is a result

The drawn Cups in 2016, 2019 and 2023 show why the holder matters. A tie can feel very different depending on who arrived carrying the trophy.
